# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- `shipcheck audit` — 9-check launch-readiness scorecard with A–F grading.
- `shipcheck listing` — App Store & Play Store listing generator with
  character limits and keyword derivation.
- `shipcheck privacy` — dependency-scan-based privacy policy and
  data-disclosure worksheet.
- `shipcheck release` — conventional-commit release-notes generator.
- `shipcheck site` — self-contained one-page marketing site generator.
